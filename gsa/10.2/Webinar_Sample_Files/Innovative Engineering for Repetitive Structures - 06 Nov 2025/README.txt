Innovative Engineering for Repetitive Structures

This folder contains two Grasshopper sample files that demonstrate different workflows from the webinar.

Requirements

- Oasys GSA 10.2
- Rhino 8
- GSA_GH plugin


Workflow

Footfall Analysis (Footfall.gh)

1. Open Footfall.gh in Rhino 8 and Grasshopper.
2. Follow the instructions provided within the Grasshopper sample file.
3. Open the following GSA seed files as instructed in the Grasshopper sample file:
   - Global Model [seed file].gwb
   - Modal Task [seed file].gwb
4. Review the footfall analysis workflow and results.

Steel Design (Steel Design.gh)

1. Open Steel Design.gh in Rhino 8 and Grasshopper.
2. Review the steel design workflow within the Grasshopper sample file.
3. View the design check results in Grasshopper.
